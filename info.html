SMART BATTERIES

Status can travel with the power.

In the research direction behind Wattwise, a battery converter can encode a small, controlled status signal in the shared DC-bus voltage. Other units already measuring that bus can detect the change and infer a state such as ready, reserve, or attention—without depending on a separate radio message.

This family of techniques is often described as bus-voltage signaling or power talk. It is a research concept for future smart-battery coordination, not a claim about the current LED prototype.
