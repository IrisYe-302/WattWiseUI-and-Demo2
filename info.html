A ladder, not a cliff.

DEVICE NEGOTIATION

Every watt makes its case.

Instead of treating a home as one anonymous load, Wattwise lets devices or smart ports describe a request: how much power they need, how urgent it is, how long it will run, and whether it can wait.

A local coordinator compares those requests with the available supply. The refrigerator keeps cooling, a protected emergency charger keeps its small draw, and entertainment standby yields first. User rules remain final; negotiation can never overrule a protected load.
